Subject: Design & Usage Approval Request | SHANLE LAB × MLBB MCT — MVP + SAVAGE

Hi team,

Thank you for your feedback and for explaining the official review process.

Please find attached our proposed SHANLE LAB × MLBB MCT event-pack sticker concept, “C — MVP + SAVAGE,” for your official review.

Design concept

The set combines the SEA Championship Tour identity with MVP and SAVAGE, two expressions familiar to MLBB players. The idea is to turn the excitement of playing and achieving memorable moments into collectible stickers that players can use on everyday items, such as phone cases.

The design features a deep navy background, teal graphics and cream-gold PU edging. The SEA event logo is the main piece, accompanied by two smaller stickers with custom MVP and SAVAGE lettering and wave-inspired decoration.

Official asset references and original design

The SEA event logo and white MLBB logo are referenced from page 5 of the supplied “SEA东南亚赛区VI手册-CN.ai.” The color direction references the color system on page 26.

As the available event-specific assets were limited, we developed custom MVP and SAVAGE lettering, sticker outlines and wave-inspired decorative treatments around the supplied event identity. We would like to confirm whether these creative adaptations are acceptable under your brand guidelines.

The attached image is a concept visualization and does not yet demonstrate an exact match to the official logo artwork. For the final production file, we will place the official logos directly from the supplied vector assets, preserving their shapes, proportions and colors, rather than tracing or recreating them. We will submit the final artwork for confirmation before production.

Please let us know if a particular logo version, minimum size, clear space, copyright notice or other brand treatment is required.

Proposed usage and distribution

The stickers are intended as physical merchandise for the MCT event packs, with a limited total quantity of 800 stickers.

Each day, we plan to provide 50 sets to the official event team for inclusion in the event packs and distribution by the organizers. Another 50 sets will be available for collection at the SHANLE LAB booth. Both allocations will be distributed on a first-come, first-served basis.

The stickers are intended as giveaways and will not be sold separately. The packaging will carry “EVENT PACK EXCLUSIVE” and “NOT FOR SALE.”

We would also like to request permission to use images of the approved products in social media posts introducing this collaboration and explaining where attendees can collect them.

Approval request

Could you please review the attached concept and confirm by email whether the design adaptations, physical production, distribution arrangements and proposed social media usage are acceptable?

Please also let us know any changes or additional documents required. We will proceed with production and promotional use only after receiving your written approval of the final artwork and usage scope.

Thank you for your guidance.
