Use case: photorealistic exhibition-booth concept
Primary request: a content-rich TUNTUN × SHANLE LAB PopSticker special booth. The previous single flat fabric wall and nearly empty counter was rejected as thin and boring. Design a compact, believable character-world shop visitors can look at, browse and try, with visual depth and REAL small products.
Reference roles:
1. Existing MLBB paper island — reuse its PRACTICAL physical counter construction only: overhanging top, recessed cardboard body, two front cupboard doors and one broad rounded printed campaign sign. Do not copy any MLBB graphics, game logos, prices or promo copy.
2. User-liked TUNTUN Snack Club product direction, small tactile PU patches with pizza/coconut/icecream original character drawings.
3. REAL physical PopSticker package — its wide flat-topped and flat-bottomed rounded backing, top central hang hole, shallow clear PET shell, two printed side arcs and SMALL size are invariant.
4. Meeko brand-manual tea-story / merchandise page — conceptual lesson ONLY: a sequential everyday character story plus the same art echoing across real objects. Do NOT use any Meeko drawings, hair, characters, logos, story text or merchandise branding.
5. Supplied original TUNTUN art — ONLY character identity. Light-blue long pointed ears, white tufts, purple eyes, white eye masks and belly, tiny heart nose. Keep recognizable original faces and poses.
Space: a plausible compact 3m-wide × 3m-deep conceptual trade-show plot, 2.4m high back wall, open front and right aisle, no raised stage. Dimensions are design assumptions, do not print them on objects. Three-quarter front-right wide photographic view in an actual indoor exhibition hall; frame entire booth including adult visitor and aisle. Make it feel genuinely 3m rather than a huge showroom.
Art direction: "TUNTUN 快乐营业中 / EVERYDAY LITTLE JOYS". Coherent ultramarine, sky blue and butter yellow with mint and little pink zones. Graphic energy with comic panels, window-shaped cutouts, diagonals and one restrained patchwork area. Professional retail art direction, layered yet organized, no sea of repeated giant oval pack pictures.
CONTENT AND DEPTH, not just wallpaper:
- Rear LEFT 1.7m-wide story wall is a physical shallow framed assemblage of four colorful comic panels at varied depths (about 3–8cm): TUNTUN notices pizza, happily eats, shares a coconut, relaxes with icecream. Big sequence arrows and tiny YUM / SLURP / CHILL speech accents tie a SMALL everyday story together. Clear main title 'TUNTUN' and small '快乐营业中'. This is large PRINTED campaign illustration, not giant sale stickers. One flat diecut 80cm-tall original TUNTUN cutout peeks beside the wall, not a stuffed sculpture.
- Rear RIGHT 1.0m-wide RETAIL MERCHANDISING WALL with 4 columns x 4 rows of actual small 147mm-wide PopSticker PET packages, three coordinated TUNTUN assortment artworks (snacks / mint mood heads / pink arcade collage). Modest real stock depth behind each. Packages stay hand-sized and occupy less than 60% width combined per row. Normal peg hooks and slim lips visible. Small headers 'SNACK', 'MOOD', 'PLAY', not oversized packs.
- LEFT short return panel contains three playful HEAD mood illustrations stacked like expression windows, with one narrow peg row below, enough visual content without crowding entrance. These are illustrations, not giant physical patches.
- CENTER foreground reuse a LOW PAPER ISLAND from reference 1, conceptual about 1.6m wide, .6m deep, .85m high. Keep both cupboard doors/handles visible and room for staff behind. A smaller 80cm-wide broad standard-shape sign offset at rear-right on this counter says 'PICK YOUR MOOD' above three original TUNTUN heads; it must NOT occlude the story wall. Leave left half tabletop clear for trying patches.
- ON THIS COUNTER: only FOUR actual small retail packages in a low rail spanning ~70cm, not a wall of large square pouches. A normal 15cm smartphone, adult over-ear headphones and a normal 30cm-wide canvas tote on adjacent different-height shallow risers. Each has ONE small 35–40mm thin PU TUNTUN patch attached, no full-object huge print. Add a sample flipbook / backing-film patch tray and small handwritten-feeling '选一枚，贴上今天的心情' sign as a simple try-it moment. Finely visible leather grain and clear PET reflections. Real small props can be visually quiet.
- RIGHT front: a little 55cm-wide stepped display cabinet supports ordinary passport holders, a phone case and small bag so the visitor sees how stickers enter everyday objects; a printed comic strip behind echoes the wall story. Do not invent a new wholesale merchandise range or giant novelty cup products.
- Floor: one simple blue shape / tiny footprints draws visitors from reading story to browse to trying at the island. Clear front-right entrance and human circulation, no clutter in aisle.
One 170cm-tall real adult visitor stands slightly left of island holding ONE tiny real PopSticker pack. Pack is only 147mm wide, ~105mm high: width 8.6% of adult height, about 37% of adult shoulder width, height less than half adult head height. The sales pack fits a natural adult handspan. Counter height around visitor hip/waist. Do NOT scale person down or product up. Printed comics and diecut sign may be large; all sellable packs, phones, headphones and bags must remain ordinary real size.
Materials: printed coated corrugated board counter with subtle folds and joins, slim modular frame with layered flat panels, no heavy glossy luxury furniture, no expensive giant sculpture. Soft real exhibition lighting, natural shadows, rich but credible small booth, perspective and scale coherent throughout. Tiny 'BOOTH CONCEPT' in lower corner only, no dimension lines or claims that construction is verified.
