Design concept
The set combines the SEA Championship Tour identity with two expressions familiar to MLBB players: MVP and SAVAGE. The aim is to turn the excitement of competing and achieving memorable moments in the game into collectible stickers that players can use on everyday items such as phone cases.

The design uses a deep navy background, teal graphics and cream-gold PU edging. The large SEA event sticker anchors the set, while the two smaller stickers feature custom MVP and SAVAGE lettering with wave-inspired decoration.
